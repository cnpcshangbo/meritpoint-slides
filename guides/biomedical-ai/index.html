RESEARCH BOUNDARY · PUT THIS ON THE POSTER

This is research training, not medical diagnosis

Correlations, classification scores, or virtual-screening rankings on public data cannot prove that a treatment works for patients. A strong project says that clearly.

  • Use only public, de-identified data licensed for research or teaching
  • Split by patient, compound, or cell line to prevent leakage
  • Report sample size, uncertainty, negative results, and failure cases
  • Separate candidate signals strictly from clinical evidence
