Where could the data come from?OPTIONAL · FOR A LATER LESSON

Your teacher can help turn your interest into a small, answerable question. These official public sources give the project somewhere concrete to start.

NHANES: oral health and demographics ↗

Anonymous questionnaire, dental examination and demographic records that can be connected by participant number.

Period
2017–2018
What is inside
OHQ030: last dental visit · OHQ620: oral pain frequency (age 30+) · OHQ640: difficulty with usual work/school (age 30+) · OHX02CTC–OHX15CTC, OHX18CTC–OHX31CTC: tooth condition · OHX03SE, OHX14SE, OHX19SE, OHX30SE: first permanent molar sealants · RIDAGEYR: age · INDFMPIR: family income-to-poverty ratio
Access
Free public XPT downloads, with no account required. NCHS permits statistical analysis/reporting and prohibits attempts to identify participants.
Joining data
SEQN
For a later lesson
Teacher-guided preparation: respect question ages, missing codes and survey weights. These cross-sectional records show associations, not causes.
File documentation: OHQ_J ↗ · OHXDEN_J ↗ · DEMO_J ↗

NHANES: first-day dietary intake ↗

Reported food intake from a 24-hour recall, including total sugar and energy.

Period
2017–2018
What is inside
DR1TSUGR: total sugars in grams · DR1TKCAL: energy in kcal · DR1DRSTZ: recall reliability status · WTDRD1: first-day dietary weight
Access
Free public XPT download; no registration. The same NCHS data-use agreement applies.
Joining data
SEQN (same NHANES cycle only)
For a later lesson
Total sugar includes naturally occurring sugar. A single recalled day does not measure a person's long-term diet.

CDC NOHSS: adult oral health summaries ↗

Ready-made state percentages for adult dental visits and tooth loss; useful for a simple map or comparison.

Period
2012–2020, even years
What is inside
year · locationabbr · indicatorid · response · data_value · data_value_type · low_confidence_interval · high_confidence_interval · break_out · break_out_category
Access
Public CDC table and CSV/API, no login. The CDC dataset's own metadata labels its license Public Domain.
Joining data
Not joined to NHANES. Compare rows with the same year, indicator, response, population breakout and estimate type.
For a later lesson
These are adult group summaries. Match the year and estimate type; group differences cannot explain an individual's outcome.

With survey data, an association is a pattern to investigate; it does not show that one factor caused another. Your teacher will help with survey weights and age groups when the project reaches that step.

How does this connect to studying dentistry?OPTIONAL · THE U.S. EDUCATION PATH
Undergraduate study→Dental school · DDS / DMD

In the U.S., students usually complete undergraduate study and dental-school prerequisites before entering a DDS or DMD program. There is no required undergraduate major; individual dental schools set their own prerequisites. Combined programs and routes outside the U.S. may differ.

A small project can help you explore an interest and practice explaining evidence. Here, the goal is to find a question you care about and keep building on it throughout the semester.
