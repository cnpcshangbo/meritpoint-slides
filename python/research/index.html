FROM COURSE PROJECT TO A POSTER

Your course outcome: a project ready for review.

Students who complete a project work toward a K–12 poster abstract based on their actual results. Before submission, a teacher reviews the evidence, student contributions and current requirements. Conference reviewers decide acceptance.

Six things to keep

  1. A clear question and a hypothesis written before testing
  2. Data sources, generation methods or permission details
  3. A reproducible notebook or code with running instructions
  4. Tables and figures comparing the baseline and your approach fairly
  5. Failure cases, limitations, student decisions and an AI-use record
  6. An abstract draft and presentation outline the student understands and has checked

Finish in December → refine in January → review before submission

The 15 scheduled classes currently end on December 20. Build the core evidence during class. Further experiments, teacher feedback and formatting in January need separate arrangements; they are not part of the scheduled 15 classes.
