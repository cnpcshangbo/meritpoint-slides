CheckRecord actual evidence
Raw sourceFile/version, collection period, population, row unit, source/terms. Preserve a raw copy.
StructureRows × columns, names, types, units. For media: filenames, labels and metadata.
MissingnessCount and denominator per relevant column. Check special codes in the codebook; missing is not zero.
Duplicates / keysFull-row repeats and proposed key repeats. Are repeats valid visits, trials or frames?
ValidityDocumented category/range rules, dates and units. Inspect suspicious source rows.
VerificationHand-check three rows, reconcile counts, rerun and verify the raw file is unchanged.
